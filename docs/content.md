## 1. Website Positioning

Jatin Mishra is an **AI-assisted website builder and business/financial analyst** with a **Bank of America risk-analytics background**. He builds fast, modern websites — often with SaaS add-ons like booking, payments, or enquiry forms — for local businesses and founders, and pairs that with genuine business and financial judgment that most freelance web builders don't have.

This is a **service landing page** built for **lead generation**: booked calls, website-audit requests, and enquiries. It is **not** an agency, **not** a corporate consultancy, **not** a passive portfolio, and **not** a senior full-stack developer portfolio. Past work is shown as *proof that supports the offer*, never as the headline.

**Audience:** schools, colleges, tuition/coaching classes, clinics, cafés, restaurants, salons and local service businesses, independent consultants, and early-stage founders — owners who are non-technical, time-poor, and want a professional web presence from someone who also understands their business.

## 2. Voice & Tone

- **First person, singular.** "I build," "I'll help," "let's talk." Never "we," "our team," or "our consultants" — Jatin is a solo expert, not an agency.
- **Plain and warm.** Speak to a busy, non-technical owner. Short sentences. Define anything technical in everyday words.
- **Confident, not boastful.** Claims are concrete and honest; no hype words, no fake seniority.
- **Outcome-framed, honestly.** Talk about what the visitor gets ("a site customers can book from") without promising specific numbers we can't prove.
- **Low-pressure.** Invite a conversation; reassure that it starts with a free call and a clear quote.

## 7. Work / Project Placeholder Copy Rules

The Work section is driven by real projects from `milestone.md`, hand-mirrored into `data/projects.ts`. **`milestone.md` is not yet provided**, so until it is:

- Use **clearly-marked placeholder entries** (e.g. `[PLACEHOLDER — Café website, Bhubaneswar]`) **during development only**. Never ship placeholders as if they were real work.
- Each card states a **deliverable** — *what was built* — and nothing more. Example allowed line: "Multi-page site with online booking and a digital menu." Example **banned** line: "Increased bookings 30%."

## 14. Honest-Claims Rules

1. **No invented outcomes or metrics.** Never state revenue growth, conversion lift, traffic increase, booking increase, no-show reduction, ROI, or any client result unless Jatin provides verifiable, attributed proof.
2. **Describe deliverables, not results.** Work copy says *what was built*, not *what it achieved*.
3. **Only résumé-stated figures** are used for Jatin's own stats (9 delivered, 15+ pipeline, 50+ analyses, 3 yrs BofA, $500B+ RWA). These are his stated experience, not promises to new clients.
4. **No inflated seniority.** "AI-assisted builder + business analyst," never "senior full-stack engineer" or "executive consultant."
5. **Packages are starting points** — always paired with "Final quote after a free call."
6. **First-person singular only.** No "we," "our team," or agency/consultancy framing.
7. **Placeholders are labelled** and never shipped as real content.
8. If real, provable metrics are supplied later, they may be added **with attribution** (e.g. "client-reported").

## 15. Banned Phrases & Banned Claims

**Banned brand / framing:**
- "StrategicAI", "StrategicAI Consulting", any invented brand name (brand is **Jatin Mishra**)
- "We", "our team", "our consultants", "our experts" (use first-person singular)
- "Agency", "consultancy", "firm", "executive consultants"
- "Senior full-stack developer/engineer", "expert engineer", "10x developer"

**Banned invented metrics / results (examples — never use unless proven & attributed):**
- "+24.5% conversion", "40% increase in direct bookings", "65% reduction in admin", "28% uplift in AOV", "5x faster onboarding"
- "Page load 0.8s", any specific performance/result number presented as a client outcome
- "Increased revenue/sales/traffic/bookings", "reduced no-shows", "boosted ROI/conversions"

**Banned trust theater:**
- Fake "Risk Analyzed" / "AI-Verified" badges
- "Trusted by" logo walls without real permission
- Fabricated testimonials or testimonial avatars

**Banned overpromises:**
- Guaranteed timelines/SLAs we can't keep ("response within 24 hours" as a promise)
- "Guaranteed results", "guaranteed growth", "we don't guess; we measure" (implies metric proof we don't have)
