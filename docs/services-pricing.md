# **Services & Pricing Framework**

## **Purpose**

This document defines the services, packages, indicative prices, timelines, and scope boundaries for Jatin Mishra's AI-assisted website and business setup services.

This is a planning/content source file. It can later be converted into a dedicated website page such as `/solutions` or `/services`.

---

# **Positioning**

I help small businesses, schools, clinics, restaurants, cafés, hotels, consultants, NGOs, and local companies build a better online presence using modern websites, AI-assisted content, simple automation, lead capture systems, and practical business tools.

This is a focused, affordable, AI-assisted execution service for business owners who want a professional online presence without dealing with technical complexity.

I use modern AI-assisted workflows to make planning, writing, design exploration, and website execution faster and more affordable. The final output is reviewed, customised, and built around the client's actual business needs.

# **Pricing Rules**

All prices below are indicative ranges. Your final quote depends on scope — pages, content, design, forms, integrations, urgency, and support needs.

Final pricing depends on:

* number of pages
* content requirements
* design complexity
* business type
* forms and integrations
* urgency
* revisions
* images and branding needs
* third-party tools
* monthly support requirements

Best value when bought as a bundle rather than as separate services.

Prices are negotiable depending on the final scope and selected services.

Domain, hosting, paid tools, third-party subscriptions, premium plugins, chatbot tools, stock images, and ad spend are usually charged separately unless included in the final quote.

---

# **Timeline Rules**

Every project takes a minimum of 4 working days.

Larger projects may take 7–25+ working days depending on scope, feedback, content availability, approvals, integrations, and revisions.

Timelines may increase if:

* content is not ready
* images are not ready
* brand direction is unclear
* many revisions are needed
* third-party access is delayed
* Google verification is pending
* domain or hosting access is unavailable

---

# **Scope Boundaries**

These services focus on practical websites, lead capture, AI-assisted content, simple automations, business profile setup, digital menus, enquiry forms, and no-code or lightweight integrations.

I do not position these packages as full custom software, large SaaS platforms, mobile apps, hotel management software, restaurant management software, or enterprise booking engines.

If a business needs a full mobile app, advanced software platform, custom booking engine, payment-heavy system, or complex backend application, that becomes a larger custom project with a longer timeline and higher cost.

## **Common Problems Restaurants & Cafés Face**

Many restaurants and cafés struggle with:

* Poor Google visibility
* Outdated or badly designed websites
* No proper online enquiry system
